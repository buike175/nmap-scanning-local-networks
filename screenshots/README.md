# Screenshots and Evidence

Add screenshots that demonstrate your own authorised lab work.

Before committing an image:
- Remove or blur public IP addresses and sensitive internal details.
- Hide personal device names, MAC addresses, usernames, and other identifiers.
- Make sure the screenshot is readable and clearly labelled.
- Add a short caption explaining what the image demonstrates.

Suggested filename: `nmap-host-discovery-redacted.png`

Do not add example screenshots that could be mistaken for results from your own lab.
